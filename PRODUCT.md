## Users

Primary: Nut processing plant owners and operators in Argentina and LATAM. They run facilities that process pecans (nogal pecán), walnuts (nogal de castilla), almonds, and other tree nuts at industrial scale. They need reliable, high-throughput machinery that minimizes kernel damage and maximizes yield.

Secondary: Agricultural cooperatives pooling harvest; industrial food manufacturers with in-house nut processing lines.

## Product Purpose

DYFMA designs and manufactures industrial machinery for nut cracking, shelling, sorting, and conveying — from standalone units to complete peel-to-pack lines. Success means a plant runs continuously with low kernel breakage, high recovery, and local technical support that speaks their language and stocks parts.

## Positioning

Argentinian engineering and local fabrication. Machines are designed in Argentina, built in Argentina, and supported from Argentina — so lead times are short, parts are on the shelf, and the engineering team knows the regional nut varieties (pecán, castilla, almendra) and harvest conditions firsthand. A European or North American competitor cannot truthfully claim this combination of proximity, crop-specific know-how, and sovereign supply chain.

## Operating Context

- Facilities run 1–2 shifts during harvest season (peak throughput is non-negotiable)
- Nut varieties vary by region: pecán (softer shell, high oil), castilla (harder, irregular), almendra (requires gentle handling)
- Downtime during harvest = direct revenue loss; local phone support and next-day parts matter more than spec-sheet maxima
- Buyers evaluate: kernel integrity %, throughput kg/h, changeover time between varieties, service network

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Portfolio: standalone crackers/shellers, optical/weight sorters, conveyors/elevators, AND complete integrated peel-to-pack lines (reception → cracking → shelling → sorting → drying → packing)
- Custom engineering per client: volume, variety mix, layout, budget
- Fabrication: CNC, welding, assembly in-house in Argentina
- Service: installation, commissioning, training, preventive maintenance contracts, spare parts stock
- No e-commerce — all sales are consultative, quote-based
- Website is a credibility + lead-gen surface (Persuade mode), not a product catalog with pricing

## Product Principles

1. **Proximity wins** — Local design, local build, local support beats imported spec sheets
2. **Kernel integrity over throughput theater** — Real yield is what the grower banks
3. **Engineered for the crop, not the catalog** — Pecán ≠ castilla ≠ almendra; machines reflect the difference
4. **One throat to choke** — Single-source accountability from reception to packed box
5. **Built to run, not to show** — Industrial durability > trade-show shine
